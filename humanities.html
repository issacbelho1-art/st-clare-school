Humanities Department

The Humanities department helps students understand society, culture and human values.

Through subjects like history, geography and social sciences, students develop awareness, empathy and critical thinking.

Our Humanities curriculum encourages students to explore global perspectives, human behaviour, citizenship and leadership through meaningful discussions and projects.

Students learn to analyse information, think independently and appreciate the diversity of cultures and societies around the world.
