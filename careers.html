Careers

Join the St Clare family

St Clare Higher Secondary School is a place of learning, care and service. We welcome dedicated people who are passionate about guiding young minds with knowledge, discipline and strong values.

Staff at St Clare share in the values of the school, working together to build a caring and inspiring learning community.

A vibrant school community

We believe that a good school is built by people who care. Our staff work together to support students, encourage excellence and create a safe, respectful and joyful environment.

If you are committed to education and service, we invite you to be part of the St Clare family.
