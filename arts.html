Creative and Performing Arts

Stepping into the spotlight

Our creative and performing arts programme gives every student an outlet for creativity and self-expression.

The arts enrich daily life at St Clare, and we encourage students to express their creative side through music, drama, performance, dance and visual arts.

Students are given opportunities to perform, create, collaborate and develop confidence through a wide range of artistic experiences.

Drama

Our drama programme helps students build confidence, creativity and communication skills. Students take part in performances, stage work and expressive activities throughout the year.

Those who enjoy the spotlight can perform on stage, while others can contribute through backstage support, sound, lighting and production.

Music

Music gives students the chance to discover rhythm, melody and performance. Students can explore singing, instruments and group music activities.

Through regular practice and school events, students learn discipline, teamwork and confidence.

Dance

Dance allows students to express themselves through movement. From cultural dance to contemporary performance, students learn coordination, confidence and stage presence.

School programmes and events give students opportunities to perform and celebrate creativity together.

Visual arts

Visual arts encourage imagination, observation and creativity. Students explore drawing, painting, craft and design through meaningful activities.

These experiences help students develop artistic confidence and appreciation for beauty, culture and expression.
