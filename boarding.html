BOARDING VISION

Reviving a tradition of residential life

St Clare Higher Secondary School has a proud history of residential boarding life. Boarding facilities are currently not operational, but future reopening plans will be announced by the school.

A valued tradition of residential life rooted in discipline, care, friendship, and holistic growth.

A valued part of the school’s heritage

Boarding was once an important part of life at St Clare Higher Secondary School, helping students grow in discipline, responsibility, friendship, and independence.

Boarding facilities are currently not operational. The school continues to value the spirit of residential learning and the strong sense of community it helped create among students.

St Clare remains committed to holistic education and continues to explore future possibilities for residential student life as the school grows.
