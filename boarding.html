BOARDING

A home away from home

Boarding at St Clare Higher Secondary School provides students with a safe, caring, and disciplined environment where they learn, grow, and build lifelong friendships.

A caring residential community where students grow in confidence, discipline, friendship, and responsibility.

Living, learning, and growing together

Boarding at St Clare provides students with a structured and supportive environment. Students learn independence, discipline, teamwork, and respect for others while living in a community guided by care and values.

Our boarding environment encourages healthy routines, study habits, prayer, recreation, and personal responsibility. Students are supported by staff who care for their academic, emotional, and personal wellbeing.

Through shared meals, evening study, activities, and friendships, boarders develop confidence, maturity, and a strong sense of belonging.
