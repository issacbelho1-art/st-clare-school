Our Boarding Houses

A home away from home

“Boarding at St Clare feels like a second family. We study, pray, play and grow together.”

- ST CLARE STUDENT

Our boarding houses offer the comfort, security and sense of community our students need to thrive.

Students live in a warm and welcoming environment where they can relax, study, build friendships and learn responsibility.

Sharing rooms and daily routines helps students understand one another, respect differences and create lasting friendships.

Discover Our Houses

Each boarding house has its own character and helps students feel a strong sense of identity, belonging and care.
