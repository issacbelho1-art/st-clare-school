Outdoor Education

Awe-inspiring experiences

Outdoor education encourages students to step out of their comfort zones, face challenges and build confidence.

At St Clare, outdoor education helps students discover courage, teamwork, resilience and leadership.

Students learn by doing. Through outdoor activities, they develop confidence, discipline and a deeper appreciation for nature.

These experiences help students become stronger, more responsible and more independent.

“Outdoor learning helps students face challenges and grow with confidence.”

Outdoor adventures in the curriculum

Term 1 - Adventure and exploration

Students take part in exciting outdoor activities that encourage teamwork, problem-solving and courage.

Each activity is designed to help students discover their strengths and learn new skills through experience.

Term 1 - Outdoor Activities

In Term 1, students take part in outdoor activities and practical learning experiences in and around Dimapur.

These activities help build confidence, teamwork, and leadership while encouraging students to learn beyond the classroom.
