Primary

Encouraging curiosity, confidence, discipline, and strong learning habits.

Growing through knowledge and values

The Primary School at St Clare Higher Secondary School lays the foundation for lifelong learning. We focus on building strong academic skills while nurturing curiosity, creativity, and confidence in every child.

Our curriculum provides a balanced education across English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, moral education, and co-curricular activities. Children are encouraged to think clearly, communicate well, and participate actively in the classroom.

Through reading, writing, group activities, projects, classroom discussion, and regular practice, students develop both knowledge and confidence. Teachers guide them with care, patience, and discipline.

We also emphasize respect, responsibility, teamwork, honesty, and good habits, helping students grow into confident and well-rounded individuals.
