BOARDING LIFE

Daily life, friendships & growth

Boarding life at St Clare is vibrant, structured, and filled with meaningful experiences that shape students into confident individuals.

Seizing every opportunity

A balanced blend of study, activities, discipline, friendships and personal growth makes every day at St Clare unique.

At St Clare, the rhythm of boarding life is built around study, discipline, prayer, recreation and rest.

Every student’s day is different, but each routine is designed to help them grow in confidence, responsibility and independence.

After school, students spend time studying, playing, joining activities, relaxing with friends and preparing for the next day.

A typical day

Morning

5.30 am - Wake up +
6.00 am - Prayer / preparation +
7.00 am - Breakfast +
8.30 am - School begins +

Afternoon

12.30 pm - Lunch +
3.30 pm - School ends +
4.00 pm - Games / recreation +

Evening

6.00 pm - Study time +
7.30 pm - Dinner +
8.30 pm - Evening study / prayer +
10.00 pm - Bedtime +
“Boarding life builds discipline, independence, friendship and character.”

Weekends

Weekends at St Clare are filled with opportunities to rest, play, participate in activities and spend meaningful time with friends.

Students enjoy a healthy balance of study, recreation, prayer, cultural activities and personal time.
