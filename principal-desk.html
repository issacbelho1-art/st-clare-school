PRINCIPAL'S DESK

A Message from the Principal

Welcome to St Clare Higher Secondary School, where education forms knowledge, character and service.

From the Principal's Desk

Welcome to St Clare Higher Secondary School.

St. Clare Hr. Sec. School is an English-medium institution established in 1987 by Holy Cross Parish, Dimapur, under the name St. Leo School. The present management of the school is entrusted to the Franciscan Clarist Congregation, a religious congregation dedicated to education and the well-being of all, especially the poor, following the teachings of Jesus Christ and the values of the Catholic Church.

Education is the most powerful tool to transform a child's future. At St. Clare Higher Secondary School, we do not merely prepare students for examinations; we prepare them for life. Together with our dedicated teachers and staff, we strive to guide every student on a journey of learning, discipline, character formation and personal growth.

Our vision is to empower students with knowledge, integrity and leadership qualities so that they may face the challenges of tomorrow with confidence and contribute meaningfully to society.

Academic Excellence

To provide a strong foundation in academics through qualified faculty, modern teaching methods and continuous assessment.

Character Building

To instill values of honesty, discipline, respect, empathy and responsibility in every student.

Holistic Development

To encourage participation in sports, arts, science and community service, helping students develop into well-rounded individuals.

Inclusive Learning

To ensure that every child, regardless of background, receives equal opportunities to learn, grow and succeed.

Facilities That Support Growth

Our campus provides an environment where students can thrive academically and personally. Facilities include smart classrooms with modern teaching aids, science and computer laboratories that encourage hands-on learning, a well-stocked library for reading and research, sports facilities that promote teamwork and healthy competition, and a clean, green and disciplined campus environment.

Beyond the Classroom

Education at St. Clare is enriched through a wide range of co-curricular activities. Students actively participate in debates, elocution, art and craft, music, science exhibitions, cultural programmes, sports events and inter-school competitions. These experiences foster leadership, teamwork, creativity and social responsibility.

St. Clare Higher Secondary School stands as a beacon of quality education, values and holistic development. Named after St. Clare of Assisi, whose life reflected simplicity, service and inner strength, our institution continues to uphold these ideals in every aspect of school life.

I warmly invite parents, guardians, alumni and well-wishers to visit our campus, interact with our teachers and experience the St. Clare spirit firsthand. Together, let us build a generation that is knowledgeable, skilled, compassionate and firmly rooted in values.

“Enter with Purpose. Leave with Knowledge.”

Sr. Principal
St Clare Higher Secondary School
