Learning Support

Supporting every learner with patience, care, confidence, and personal attention.

Helping every student move forward

At St Clare Higher Secondary School, we believe that every student learns differently. Our Learning Support programme is designed to help students strengthen their understanding, overcome difficulties, and grow with confidence.

Teachers identify areas where students may need additional guidance and provide support through regular follow-up, remedial classes, small-group learning, and one-on-one attention where needed.

The programme focuses on building strong foundations in key subjects such as English, Mathematics, Science, and other core areas. Students are also guided in study habits, revision methods, time management, and classroom confidence.

Our goal is not only to improve marks, but to help each child believe in their ability to learn, participate, and succeed.
