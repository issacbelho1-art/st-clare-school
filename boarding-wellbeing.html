Health & Wellbeing

Supporting every student

We care deeply about the physical, emotional and spiritual wellbeing of every student in our boarding community.

At St Clare, wellbeing is at the heart of everything we do.

Our boarding environment is designed to provide a safe, caring and structured space where students feel supported and valued.

From dedicated house parents to pastoral care and guidance, every student receives attention and encouragement to grow with confidence.

Pastoral care

Each student is supported by house parents and staff who guide them in their daily life, ensuring they feel secure and heard.

Health support

We provide access to medical care and ensure students maintain a healthy lifestyle through balanced routines and activities.

Emotional wellbeing

Students are encouraged to express themselves, build strong relationships and develop resilience in a nurturing environment.

“A safe and supportive environment helps every student thrive.”
