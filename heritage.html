Our Heritage

Our Journey Since 1987

Established in 1987, St Clare Higher Secondary School has grown with faith, dedication and service to the community of Kacharigaon (Phevima), Dimapur, Nagaland.

From its early years, the school has focused on forming students with knowledge, discipline and strong values. Over time, St Clare has become a place where children learn, grow and prepare for a meaningful future.

Our heritage is built on commitment, care and the belief that education should shape both the mind and character of every child.
