About Us

St Clare Higher Secondary School

Established in 1987, St Clare Higher Secondary School has been serving the community of Kacharigaon (Phevima), Dimapur, Nagaland with dedication, discipline and care.

The school believes in forming young minds through quality education, moral values and a nurturing environment. Over the years, St Clare has grown as a place where students learn, discover their talents and prepare themselves for a responsible future.

Our aim is to help every student grow in knowledge, character and confidence, while remaining rooted in respect, service and responsibility.

Our Heritage

Since its establishment in 1987, St Clare Higher Secondary School has continued to serve families of Kacharigaon (Phevima), Dimapur and nearby communities with commitment and care.

Our Vision

To form confident, disciplined and responsible students with a strong foundation in knowledge, values and service.

Our Mission

To provide a caring and inspiring learning environment where every child grows academically, morally and personally.

Our Commitment

At St Clare Higher Secondary School, we are committed to nurturing students with care, discipline and strong values, preparing them for a meaningful future.
