LEARNING AT ST CLARE

Unlocking passion and potential

Our education is designed to nurture individual passions and ambitions. Students excel academically while enjoying meaningful learning experiences.

A St Clare education is rich, diverse and tailored to individual passions and ambitions.

Our students excel in their chosen academic subjects, enjoy life-changing learning experiences beyond the classroom, and develop the skills and mindset they need to thrive.

Our personalised approach to education is underpinned by globally respected curricula. We ensure that every learner receives the right level of challenge and support.

St Clare Higher Secondary School is committed to providing quality education that builds strong academic foundations, moral values, and discipline.

Our teaching approach focuses on critical thinking, creativity, and holistic development. We aim to prepare students not only for examinations but for life.
