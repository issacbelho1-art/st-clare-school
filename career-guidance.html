Career & University Guidance

Helping students discover their future pathways, build confidence, and make informed decisions for higher education and careers.

Personalised Career Support

At St Clare Higher Secondary School, we guide students in identifying their strengths, interests, and aspirations. Our dedicated mentors work closely with students to help them explore career options and make informed decisions.

Through one-on-one counselling, aptitude guidance, and structured planning, students gain clarity about their future goals and academic pathways.

University Preparation

We prepare students for higher education by providing support in university selection, application processes, and entrance preparation. Students receive guidance on building strong academic profiles and preparing essential documents.

Our programme ensures students are confident and well-prepared to pursue opportunities both nationally and internationally.
